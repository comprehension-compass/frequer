# Spanish exclusion list, 2026-11-01

`corpora/exclude/es.tsv` drops English leakage and website boilerplate from the es snapshot
(`frequer/exclude.py`). The snapshot was rebuilt from the same counts (105,726,244 tokens, same runtime
header); nothing was recounted.

**Criterion** (the developer's words): remove English words and boilerplate, but don't remove words that
are borrowed into Spanish and adapted, to the point that an English-speaking learner would want to learn
that they're used in Spanish.

## Candidates (1,731)

* every lemma in the first build's foreign-word table;
* every top-50k lemma whose wordfreq English zipf is >= 2.5 and exceeds its Spanish zipf by >= 0.8
  (by >= 1.2 with English zipf >= 5.0 for PROPN-tagged lemmas, so English words mistagged as names
  such as *the* and *city* come in, while ordinary names don't);
* boilerplate: the flight-search site's brand and template spellings (*jetcost*, *aereo*, *tiquete*),
  and non-PROPN unaccented spellings whose accented form ranks with at least 3x the frequency;
* unit headwords made of English-leaning words.

## Review

Two independent reviewers saw only the criterion, the lemma, its rank, frequency, POS and two example
sentences from the counted sources (ParaCrawl's head, Tatoeba). Neither saw the other's answers. They
agreed on 1,650 of 1,731; an adjudicator settled the other 81. The per-lemma record, with both
reviewers' classes, is in `exclusions-review.tsv`.

| keep-loan | 311 |

The build also dropped 3 unit headwords whose words are all excluded: *human rights watch*, *long island*,
*one piece*. That is 1,245 dropped from the top 50k in all.

* Rank 50,000 is now at frequency 23 (was 24); rank 20,000 at 125 (was 131).
* Top 10k: 476 lemmas keep their rank and 9,524 move up (median 67 ranks; 4,189 by more than 100);
  223 lemmas are new to the top 10k.
* `load_lemmas es --path snapshots/es.json --dry-run`: 50,000 ranked entries, 50,000 new, 0 demoted,
  0 skipped; 1,662 unit headwords, 51 homograph keys.
