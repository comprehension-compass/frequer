# question Verbinden: there are no examples to show whether it is the button-label verb or the noun "das Verbinden". The genitive "verbindens" hints at noun use, but I merged to the verb.
# question Nichten: the real plural of Nichte exists, but the evidence is spam text using "nichtens" for "nicht". I dropped it; if Nichte should be taught, it needs a separate entry.
