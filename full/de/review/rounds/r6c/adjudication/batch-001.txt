ssen	drop	broken fragment with no examples; the source could be essen, müssen, wissen or lassen, so there is no obvious target
Knacken	keep	nominalised verb that is a noun in its own right (das Knacken), like Essen
Offizielle	merge:offiziell	inflected adjective capitalised in a title ("Der Offiziellen Seite"), not a noun
Deg	merge:Degen	cut-off fragment; nearly all forms are degen, a real noun, even though the one example is a surname
befassten	merge:sich befassen	conjugated form of a pronominal verb; all examples use "befassten sich"
Bundesrechtsanwaltskammer	drop	specialist professional body, not a famous institution a learner meets
rang	drop	no examples; mixes English range/ranger with rang (ringen or Rang), so there is no clear target
angesammelt	merge:ansammeln	participle forms; transitive ansammeln also exists ("das angesammelte Wissen")
Wett	merge:wettmachen	almost always the detached particle of wettmachen ("macht das wett"), not a cut of Wette
nachrichten	merge:Nachrichten	lower-case noun; the plural has its own meaning (the news)
Twister	drop	game brand and band name, not an established loan
eingeschüchtert	merge:einschüchtern	participle without its own dictionary entry
bie	drop	garbled forms (bie, bieden, bien), apparently Dutch or French; no clear German target
merkur	drop	lower-case name (newspaper, URL or planet) with no examples
Mem	merge:Meme	plural and variant forms of the established loan Meme
Glucose	keep	accepted German technical spelling, not a broken or English-only form
erstenmal	merge:Mal	pre-1996 spelling of "zum ersten Mal"; the lemma is broken
bes	drop	mixed garble (bes, beser, besste); possibly the abbreviation bes. (besonders), with no evidence
Nasch	merge:Naschen	every example is the nominalised noun (das Naschen); the lemma is a cut-off stem
bewahrheitet	merge:sich bewahrheiten	conjugated form of a pronominal verb ("haben sich bewahrheitet")
bemehlten	merge:bemehlen	inflected participle form of bemehlen
mutt	merge:Mutter	written forms are overwhelmingly mutter; the English look-alike is minor
anhin	keep	real Swiss adverb (bis anhin) with its own dictionary entry
sep	merge:September	abbreviation of a listed word (Sep in dates)
Volkszeitung	drop	used only in regional newspaper titles, not famous institutions
Zollverein	keep	famous World Heritage site and important historical term (Deutscher Zollverein)
beigeordnet	merge:Beigeordnete	nominalised adjective noun (der/die Beigeordnete) filed as a lower-case participle
Luch	merge:Luchs	written forms and all examples are Luchs
befragt	merge:Befragte	lower-case form of the noun der/die Befragte, as in the example
Fer	merge:fern	cut-off fragment; most written forms are fern
