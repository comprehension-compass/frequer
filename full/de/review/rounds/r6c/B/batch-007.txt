# question Konstanzer: demonyms are not place names themselves; I treated them as place-derived and dropped them (same for Leverkusener, Ingolstädter). Confirm they are not meant to be kept as learnable adjectives or nouns.
# question Niese: the examples show nominalised Niesen (noun) rather than the verb. I merged into the verb niesen as the clearly broken lemma. Should it be merge:Niesen instead?
# question Ver: the fragment (ver, vern) has no obvious source word, so I dropped it although fragments are normally merged.
# question nehmend: the forms (nehmende, nehmenden…) could come from zunehmend, abnehmend, annehmend and others. No obvious target, so I dropped it.
# question nnen: the fragment has no examples and no obvious source, so I dropped it.
# question int: the fragment has no examples and no obvious source, so I dropped it.
# question WISSEN: the written form is lower-case wissen. I merged into the verb, but the noun Wissen is also plausible.
# question Rit: Riten is the plural of Ritus, but rit and rites do not fit that lemma. I merged into Ritus anyway.
# question mist: the written forms include mister (English "Mr"), which does not belong under Mist. I merged into Mist for the main form.
# question tantra: this may count as a specialist foreign word. I merged it into Tantra as a noun.
