# question Mose: the lemma is a biblical name and also a book title (3. Mose); I treated it as a name and dropped it.
# question Heidelberger: the rules cover place names but not demonyms; I dropped all the -er demonyms (Heidelberger, Aachener, Bochumer, Mannheimer) as place-derived proper forms.
# question Hyundai: it is a brand, and the rules do not say whether a brand counts as a famous institution; I dropped it, as I did Lenovo.
# question plan: an adjective plan (flat) exists, but the forms plane/plans/plan look like the noun Plan or the verb planen; I merged into Plan.
# question personal: Personal and Personaler are both nouns, and an adjective personal also exists; I picked Personaler because every example uses it.
# question IM: it is unclear whether im is itself a lemma or should be merged into in; I merged to im.
# question jo: it could be a regional particle meaning "yes" and so a keep; I dropped it as non-standard.
