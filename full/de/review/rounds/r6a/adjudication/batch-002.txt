# question Glut, schnellen, ausreden, gravieren, anpfeifen, sachverständig, Ober: these were kept as real words, but most of their counts belong to another word (Gluten, Schnelltest, Ausrede, gravierend, Anpfiff, Sachverständiger, ober). Should those counts be re-lemmatised or split?
# question WG: the rules call for merging "an abbreviation of a listed word", but WG is the everyday word itself. Do everyday abbreviations like WG, Kita and LKW stay keep?
# question gr: the bare form "gr" (1,919) has no examples and could partly be the abbreviation of Gramm. Is merge:groß acceptable on the evidence of grer/gres/gren?
# question nichtmal: the misspelling stands for two words (nicht mal / nicht einmal). Is merging into one part (mal) right, or should a two-word target be allowed?
# question Jupiter: are planet and other celestial names (Jupiter, Mars, Saturn) "other names" to drop, or learnable common words?
# question personal: the Personaler counts (800) were merged into Personal (staff). Should Personaler be its own lemma instead?
