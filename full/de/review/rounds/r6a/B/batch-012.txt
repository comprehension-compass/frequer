# question Münchener: demonym derived from a place name; the rules cover place names but not derived adjectives, so I dropped it (same for Basler).
# question Glut: written forms are mostly Gluten (the protein, a noun in its own right), but the lemma Glut is also a real word; I kept it.
# question Aal: counts are dominated by the city Aalen, but the lemma Aal is a valid noun; I kept it.
# question ben: forms best/ben/benen/bte may come from üben or another verb, but no source word is obvious; I dropped it.

# question Metro: no examples to confirm whether it is the transit loan or the company name; I kept it.
# question durchfahren: written forms are mixed with durchführen; I kept it as a verb.
# question fehl: it may be a prefix rather than a standalone lemma and is mixed with Fehler; I kept it.
