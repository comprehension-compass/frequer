# zh: word samples per band, for the cutoff

Positions are places in the cleaned list (kept lemmas only). The developer chooses the cutoff from these words and the comparison across languages; this file decides nothing. Glosses are written by a model as a reading aid.

From the cleaned list as it would load after review rounds 1-10 (every flagged lemma inside 40.5k reviewed by two reviewers and an adjudicator; the unflagged keeps screened across the whole window and again after each re-route; zh rulings and calls in `full/zh/junk-review.md`). Not a snapshot. Corpus: **818,629,336 tokens** (HPLT v2 zho_Hans 818.3M, Tatoeba cmn 0.35M; Simplified only). Kept rank 40,000 = raw rank 44,739 (f 726).

Two zh conventions to read the words by: a key after `#` is part of the lemma (a reading such as 行#xíng, a sense such as 花#spend, #comp for a complement, #mw for a measure word); names stay where they are learnable things (lemmatiser ruling R12: places, holidays, platforms, famous institutions), people drop.
