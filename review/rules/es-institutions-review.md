# Famous institutions: review rules (the developer's ruling; binding)

Each lemma below was dropped from Honeyeater's Spanish learner word list as a name. A screener thought it might
name a famous institution. The developer's ruling, for all languages: **famous institutions that aren't
places are kept** as learnable: nationally famous universities, ministries, broadcasters and public bodies people
refer to by name (UNAM, RENFE, RTVE, the Real Academia Española and the like). Ordinary names still drop:
people, minor companies, brands, products.

Decide exactly one per lemma:
- `keep`: the lemma (as written in the examples) is the name or the usual acronym of an institution a learner of
  Spanish will meet by name: famous in the Spanish-speaking world (a country's national universities, ministries,
  broadcasters, rail and postal services, tax and police bodies, central banks, national academies, public health
  services) or a major international body Spanish refers to by name (ONU, OTAN, UE, OMS, Unesco, FMI, FIFA,
  NASA, Interpol). Most occurrences in the examples must be that institution.
- `drop`: anything else — an institution famous only outside the Spanish-speaking world and rarely named in
  Spanish news, an ordinary company or brand, a political party, a sports club or league, a museum or venue (a
  place), a person, a product, a word that is only one piece of a longer name (unless the piece is how people
  name it, like "Complutense"), or a token whose examples are mostly something else.

When it is a close call, decide anyway and add `# question <lemma>: ...`.
