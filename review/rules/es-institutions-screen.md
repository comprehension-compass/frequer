# Famous-institutions screen

These lemmas were dropped from Honeyeater's Spanish learner word list as names. A new developer ruling:
**famous institutions that aren't places are kept** as learnable: nationally famous universities, ministries,
broadcasters and public bodies people refer to by name (UNAM, RENFE, RTVE, the Real Academia Española and the
like), and major public or international bodies a learner meets in Spanish news (e.g. Hacienda, Guardia Civil,
Seguridad Social, ONCE, OTAN, UE, OMS, Unesco). Ordinary names still drop: people, minor or ordinary companies,
brands, products, sports clubs, websites, events, places.

Your job is only to SCREEN: list every lemma that is, or might be, the name (or the acronym, or one word of the
name) of such a famous institution. Be generous: a later full review decides. Skip everything else.

Output one line per pick: `lemma<TAB>institution it names`. Lemmas are lowercased; the forms column shows how
they are written.
