# Triage rules (screening only)

These lemmas are on Honeyeater's Spanish learner word list and passed every automatic filter. A blind check
found a few per cent of these are still junk. Your job is only to SCREEN. Suspects get a full review with
example sentences later, so when in doubt, list it as a suspect.

A lemma is a suspect when it looks like any of these:
- a word from another language (Italian, Catalan, Portuguese, French, English, Latin…) rather than Spanish or an
  established loan;
- an inflected or misspelt form filed as a lemma (a plural, a feminine, a conjugated verb, a superlative, a missing
  accent, a verb with a clitic attached), when the dictionary form differs;
- a name (person, brand, company, product, place) or a multi-word name;
- an abbreviation, code, fragment or web artefact;
- not a word at all.

A real Spanish word or established loan in its dictionary form is fine, even if rare or specialised.

Below are lemmas (lemma, POS tag, how it is written in text). Answer with one suspect per line:
`<lemma>\t<few-word reason>`, the lemma copied exactly. If there are none, answer `none`. Nothing else.
